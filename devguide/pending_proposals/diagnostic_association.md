# Controlled diagnostic association trial

## What

Execute the comparison selected by `uibcdf/recorda#15`. Use public explicit
operations and application-owned SMonitor scopes/handlers; keep native dummy
science and Recorda runtime unchanged.

## How / evidence

The issue precedes this report. Use published SMonitor 0.19.0 py_1 and
pytest-receptor 1.1.0 on Python 3.14. Qualify a frozen installed pair and actual
appropriate CI before completing the receiving decision.

## Why

Attempt identity, scientific outcome, delivered selected diagnostics and native
artifact retention answer separate questions. A successful retry cannot rewrite
the prior failed attempt or prove complete warning observation.

## Alternatives

Compare a reviewed application-retained native SMonitor bundle with a bounded
consumer-owned association sidecar, both referenced through existing Recorda APIs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Independent scientific and diagnostic oracles; exact native objects/errors.
- Attempt/reference ownership and safe fixed selected fields.
- Attachment, tasks/threads, late events, aggregation/filtering and feedback.
- Bounds, missing/altered artifacts, interrupted recording and ordinary faults.
- Installed pair, published receptors and executed appropriate hosted checks.

## Resolution

Local installed qualification passes 360 tests/four Sabueso skips, 27 new cases,
seven selected notebooks/55 code cells and 14 real-kernel fault cells. The trial's
initial catalog replacement defect was corrected by preserving existing public
declarations and is covered by a receiving regression. Source/wheel/provider
hashes are retained in `../evidence/diagnostic_association_linux_py314.json`.
Hosted exact-pair qualification remains pending. Provider limitations remain
`uibcdf/smonitor#40`, `uibcdf/smonitor#41`, `uibcdf/smonitor#42`.
